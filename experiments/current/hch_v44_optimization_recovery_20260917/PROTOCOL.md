## 1. Purpose

Recover the optimization path of the already-frozen v4.4 G2 object after the first registered recipe produced a near-identity map on most TEST cells. This stage is **not** a new architecture stage and is **not** allowed to tune on V2 TEST.

The primary question is narrow:

> Does a step-based training budget with sufficient burn-in allow the existing v4.4 G2 object to produce non-trivial, VAL-improving corrections across markets?

## 7. R1 — step-budget recovery

### 7.1 Fixed recipe

- optimizer: AdamW;
- learning rate: `1e-3`;
- batch size: `32`;
- weight decay: `1e-4`;
- EMA decay: `0.995`;
- evaluation weights: EMA only;
- max optimizer steps: **2000**;
- validation interval: **50 optimizer steps**;
- **step 0 remains a legal candidate checkpoint** for honest comparison;
- early stopping is disabled before **800 optimizer steps**;
- after step 800, patience = **8 validation checks** (=400 optimizer steps);
- earliest possible stop therefore occurs at step 1200 if no later improvement exists;
- hard stop at step 2000;
- TRAIN-only difficulty interleaving remains exact-once-per-epoch and unchanged.

The point is to give every market sufficient optimizer updates regardless of TRAIN-set size while keeping the original optimizer and initialization.

### 7.2 What is logged every validation check

- step;
- epoch-equivalent/sample exposures;
- EMA VAL MAE;
- raw-weight VAL MAE as diagnostic only;
- TRAIN reconstruction MAE on a frozen diagnostic subset;
- `mean|correction| / mean|residual|` on TRAIN and VAL;
- Level MAE, B MAE, Shape W1 auxiliary;
- gradient norms for Level/B/Shape/shared encoders from the registered diagnostic batch;
- learning rate;
- selected-checkpoint flag.

Raw-vs-EMA may never be chosen outcome-conditionally; EMA is primary.

### 7.3 R1 selection

Select the EMA checkpoint with minimum VAL MAE over **all** validation checkpoints including step 0. This preserves the honest possibility that the identity-like checkpoint is truly best after sufficient training; the recovery stage must not force a non-zero correction if VAL does not support it.

## 8. R1 promotion gate on the 8-cell panel

Using seed-median VAL metrics:

1. recovered R1 improves over the frozen v4.4 recipe in at least `6/8` cells;
2. panel median improvement vs frozen v4.4 is at least `0.5%`;
3. at least `5/8` cells obtain their selected checkpoint at step > 0 and have selected-checkpoint `mean|c|/mean|r| >= 0.05`;
4. worst degradation vs frozen v4.4 is no worse than `1.0%`;
5. no leakage/provenance/numerical failure.

If PASS, freeze R1 and expand **the exact same recipe** to the remaining 12 cells × 3 seeds. Reuse the 24 panel fits; total R1 full-panel count becomes exactly 60, not 84.

## 9. Full China-5 TRAIN+VAL recovery gate

On all 20 market×Host cells, seed-median VAL:

- >=15/20 non-worse vs frozen v4.4;
- >=12/20 improve by at least 0.5%;
- panel-median VAL gain vs the same Host is >0 and materially above the frozen recipe;
- no market has median degradation worse than 1.0%;
- no cell-specific recipe or seed selection.

If PASS, emit `HCH_V44_OPTIMIZATION_RECOVERY_READY_FOR_UNTOUCHED_CONFIRMATION` and stop. Do not open TEST.

## 10. Conditional R2 — only if R1 fails for optimization, not generalization

R2 is permitted only when the R1 panel fails promotion **and** diagnostics show a clear optimization-limited signature rather than a VAL-generalization failure.

### 10.1 Optimization-limited trigger

At least one must hold in >=50% of the 24 R1 runs:

- selected step is the 2000-step ceiling;
- TRAIN reconstruction is still improving over the final 400 steps while VAL has not formed a stable minimum;
- selected/live correction magnitude remains <5% of residual magnitude despite non-zero gradients;
- EMA visibly lags a substantially better raw VAL trajectory for >400 steps.

If R1 produces non-trivial corrections and TRAIN converges but VAL simply does not improve, **R2 is forbidden** and v4.4 recovery stops.

### 10.2 R2a — learning-rate acceleration, preferred first

Keep architecture and initialization identical to R1. Change only:

- `lr: 1e-3 -> 3e-3`;

All other step/EMA/stopping rules remain identical. Run only the same 8-cell panel × 3 seeds. Select by VAL only.

Rationale: zero Level/Mass readout blocks upstream gradients for only the first update; persistent slow optimization is more plausibly a step-size/budget issue than a semantic initialization issue.

### 10.3 R2b — tiny readout activation, only if R2a is not indicated or fails activation diagnostics

If the diagnostic specifically shows readout activation remains the bottleneck, authorize exactly one initialization change:

- Level final affine weight: small random `N(0,1e-3)` instead of exact zero;
- Level bias remains zero;
- balanced-mass semantics remain unchanged (`~0.01*s_B`);
- all other architecture/loss/training settings stay as R1.

No target-derived/oracle warm start.

R2a and R2b are never combined in the same first comparison. Combining them would require a new adjudication.

### 10.4 R2 promotion

Same 8-cell promotion gate as R1. If one R2 path passes, freeze exactly that path and run the full 20-cell TRAIN+VAL panel. If neither passes, stop recovery with `HCH_V44_OPTIMIZATION_RECOVERY_NOT_SUPPORTED`.
