## 5. Next Steps (top 3-5 issues)

1. CRC severely degrades (+17.89 MAE) — safety gates too aggressive or Ridge+LightGBM ensemble unstable on electricity price data. Needs per-dataset gate tuning or exclusion.
2. HCH abstains on 57/85 combos (67%). The primary limitation is low neg_n in most datasets — SCARR cannot certify λ>0 with <10 calibration events. The abstention is correct behaviour, but limits paper's headline numbers.
3. QuantileCorrection and SpikeRegularization achieve strong aggregate Δ-MAE (-6.91/-5.50) but have zero safety mechanism — they modify predictions universally, causing normal-regime degradation on some combos. This is a key advantage HCH should emphasize in the paper.
4. HCH pos branch λ_pos=0 on ALL 85 combos — positive spike correction is never certified. Likely because S1 p99 threshold leaves too few S4 spike events (spike_n < 10 on most datasets) for SCARR to certify.
5. shandong dominates HCH improvement — contributes almost all of the -0.71 mean Δ-MAE. Paper argument should focus on "correct where events exist" rather than aggregate numbers across 0-event datasets.
