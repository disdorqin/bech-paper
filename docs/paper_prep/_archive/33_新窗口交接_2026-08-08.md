## 4. P0 episode evidence: valid problem anchor

File: `experiments/05-episode-audit/results/P0_DECISION.md`.

- 1,945 negative-price episodes and 10,300 event hours.
- 83.4% last at least 2 hours; 59.6% last at least 4 hours.
- Frozen day-ahead bases often completely miss events: approximately 82% for Linear and 92% for GBDT in the audited setting.
- Scientific conclusion: negative prices are episode-structured and complete-event misses are a real failure mode.
- This is a problem-anchor GO, not an algorithm-novelty GO.
